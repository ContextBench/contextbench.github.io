# Native grading and artifact review details

These notes preserve the detailed policies and release-review disclosures for the
[500-task CodeNib submission](README.md). They apply to all results in this folder.

## Native grading and failure handling

Each new solver generates its own candidate patch. Native grading receives that
unchanged first-attempt final patch; reference fixes, test patches and grading feedback
stay outside model inputs. Native environment/source recovery and neutral/reference
controls add no candidate score. All original failed controls and recovery records
remain archived.

The original pinned Verified, Pro, Poly and Multi criteria are retained with the
recorded native resource/source profiles and explicit conservative negative policies.
All 116 Poly and 153 remaining Multi source environments were admitted before their paid
attempts. The Multi admission binds 306 actual paired control records and 28
independently executed baselines. Poly has 113 strict positive-reference cases and three
declared identifier defects retained as conservative negatives. Native-only image
caches, public dependencies, memory, CPU/thread/process/worker settings, networking,
deadlines and log capture are recorded separately from inference; all 19 native runtime
source files per new case are retained and authenticated.

Native positives mean the pinned required criteria passed; they do not imply every
repository test passed or the issue was independently proven fixed. A recorded
django-7530 baseline-positive task remains included. Pro base-source/test-patch
conflicts, the collection-blocked pylint case and conservative Poly negatives retain
their original unsuccessful outcomes and missing execution evidence. They are not recast
as executed behavioral failures. Separate raw stdout/stderr and their composition are
preserved for the admitted Logstash profile; cross-stream chronology is unavailable.

## Raw evidence and redactions

`logs/official-metrics.json` is the original official per-instance context output, and
`logs/official-evaluation.log` retains evaluation diagnostics.
`logs/joined-results.jsonl` retains native outcomes and unique-session fees.
`logs/repair-index.jsonl`, `logs/evaluation-input.json`, `logs/protocol.json` and the
archive manifests bind cases, original patch/receipt hashes, evaluation inputs and
versions. The raw stage-specific verification files keep their original scope and
readiness flags; later checks are separate rather than rewriting earlier records.

All actual raw interactions and native logs are committed in 69 bounded archive files
under `trajectories/`. Five repair cohorts contain 58 + 64 + 55 + 54 + 269 disjoint
candidate records, while the search cohort contains all 1,000 original workers. The
archive indexes list every member, checksum and byte count. Repair streams are
zstd-compressed TARs split into ordered parts; concatenate their listed parts before
decompression. The ten search files are independent gzip TARs and must be extracted
individually rather than concatenated. Each committed file is at most 50 MiB. These are
actual files, not Git LFS pointers or external-link substitutes.

Known workstation filesystem labels and opaque SDK UUIDs are normalized or pseudonymized
consistently. Original-before/public-after commitments and session-digest links preserve
verification. Model requests, source observations, patches, selections, fees and native
outcomes retain their verified semantics. The current operator credential is excluded;
matched credential values are never printed or stored in audit reports. Original
code/object bytes and public test data remain intact where needed for source
verification.

The 269-task increment scan reviews 44,618 publication members and 1,704,188 unique
decompressed Git objects. Its 102 declared-pattern findings are all bound to complete
anonymous immutable public source or the complete pinned public native CSV: 51
test/example TLS keys, 37 public-source literal collisions with the workstation
hostname, 7 editor test-input strings, 2 published upstream application authorization
constants, and 5 public-native-dataset markers. The two application constants are
preexisting yt-dlp extractor source, not operator or user-session credentials; exact
source URLs/commits and the retention decision are disclosed in
`logs/verification/remaining269-classifications.json`. Prior 55 / Pro54 declared
findings are independently classified; prior 58 / 64 scans record no remaining matches.
This is a contextual, declared-pattern and known-operator review, not a guarantee to
detect arbitrary secrets or all personal data. Canonical upstream source, including
public authorship and fixtures, is retained as provenance. No unselected native-dataset
row bodies were rendered or supplied to a model during the opaque full-file comparison.
